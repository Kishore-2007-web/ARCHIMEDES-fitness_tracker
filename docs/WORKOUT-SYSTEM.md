# ARCHIMEDES — Workout System Specification

## 1. Core Weekly Schedule

The ARCHIMEDES training regimen is structured around athletic strength, joint mobility, kinetic reaction, relative calisthenics, and continuous walking.

Combat drills, weapons practice, and martial arts movements are strictly prohibited within this system.

---

### MONDAY — Squat + Bench Strength A
- **Base XP**: 300 XP
- **Target Attributes**: `STR`, `DIS`, `FOC`
- **Estimated Duration**: 90–120 minutes

#### Preparation Block (30 Minutes)
- 5 min easy walking & joint movement
- Ankle mobility & deep squat hold
- 90/90 hip switches & hip circles
- World's Greatest Stretch & thoracic rotation
- Shoulder mobility & scapular push-ups
- Agility footwork & lateral transitions
- Empty-bar squat & bench warm-up

#### Main Training
1. **Barbell Back Squat** — 5 × 3–5 reps (`WEIGHT_REPS`, Benchmark)
2. **Barbell Bench Press** — 5 × 3–5 reps (`WEIGHT_REPS`, Benchmark)
3. **Machine / Cable Row** — 3 × 8–10 reps (`WEIGHT_REPS`)
4. **Romanian Deadlift** — 3 × 6–8 reps (`WEIGHT_REPS`)
5. **Farmer Carry** — 3 × 30–45 sec (`WEIGHT_DURATION`)
6. **Dead Hang** — 2 × near-max comfortable hold (`DURATION`)

#### Recovery Block (15 Minutes)
- Hip-flexor, hamstring, calf, chest, and lat stretches
- Thoracic mobility & slow diaphragmatic breathing

#### Walking Block
- 30–45 minutes continuous walking

---

### TUESDAY — Mobility + Agility + Athleticism
- **Base XP**: 200 XP
- **Target Attributes**: `AGI`, `MOB`, `END`, `DIS`
- **Estimated Duration**: 90 minutes

#### Preparation & Mobility (30 Minutes)
- Lower: Knee-to-wall, ankle circles, calf raises, deep squat hold, 90/90, Cossack squat, hip-flexor stretch
- Dynamic: Leg swings, hamstring sweeps, World's Greatest Stretch
- Upper: Cat-cow, thoracic rotations, wall slides, external rotation, scapular push-ups, wrist circles

#### Agility & Athleticism
1. **Jump Rope** — 10 minutes continuous cadence (`DURATION`)
2. **Reaction Ball Drill** — 10 minutes kinetic reaction (`DURATION`)
3. **Agility Footwork Drill** — 10 minutes lateral transitions (`DURATION`)

#### Recovery & Walking
- 15 minutes static stretching & breathing
- 30–45 minutes continuous walking

---

### WEDNESDAY — Deadlift + Full Body Strength (Day 1)
- **Base XP**: 300 XP
- **Target Attributes**: `STR`, `END`, `DIS`
- **Estimated Duration**: 90–120 minutes

#### Preparation Block (30 Minutes)
- 5 min general warm-up, 10 min mobility, 5 min agility, 10 min deadlift movement-specific prep

#### Primary Movement
1. **Conventional Deadlift** — 4 × 3 reps (`WEIGHT_REPS`, Benchmark)

#### Support Movements
2. **Leg Press** — 3 × 8–10 reps (`WEIGHT_REPS`)
3. **Lat Pulldown** — 3 × 8–10 reps (`WEIGHT_REPS`)
4. **Dumbbell Bench Press** — 3 × 8 reps (`WEIGHT_REPS`)
5. **Back Extension** — 2 × 10–12 reps (`BODYWEIGHT_REPS`)

#### Grip & Controlled Neck Protocol
6. **Grip Trainer** — 3 sets to controlled fatigue (`BODYWEIGHT_REPS`)
7. **Neck Flexion** — 2 × 15 reps (`BODYWEIGHT_REPS`, Controlled motion, zero aggressive loading)
8. **Neck Extension** — 2 × 15 reps (`BODYWEIGHT_REPS`, Controlled motion)
9. **Neck Side Bend** — 2 × 15 reps/side (`BODYWEIGHT_REPS`, Controlled motion)

#### Recovery & Walking
- 15 min hamstrings, hips, glutes, lats, and thoracic decompression
- 30–45 minutes continuous walking

---

### THURSDAY — Athletic + Mobility
- **Base XP**: 200 XP
- **Target Attributes**: `AGI`, `END`, `MOB`
- **Estimated Duration**: 90 minutes

#### Preparation (30 Minutes)
- Joint mobility, dynamic flexibility, movement activation

#### Agility Block
1. **Jump Rope** — 15 minutes (`DURATION`)
2. **Reaction Ball Drill** — 10 minutes (`DURATION`)
3. **Footwork Drill** — 15 minutes (`DURATION`)

#### Conditioning (10–15 Minutes)
4. **Conditioning Choice** (`DURATION`): Rowing machine, stationary bike, elliptical, or brisk walk. *(Treadmill is not required).*

#### Recovery & Walking

- **Target Attributes**: `STR`, `DIS`, `FOC`

#### Support & Forearms

4. **Plank Hold** — 3 × 30–60 sec (`DURATION`, Benchmark)
5. **Hanging Knee Raise** — 3 × 8–15 reps (`BODYWEIGHT_REPS`)

#### Athletic & Grip Block
6. **Jump Rope** — 10–15 minutes (`DURATION`)
7. **Reaction Ball** — 10 minutes (`DURATION`)
8. **Farmer Carry** — 3 rounds × 30–45 sec (`WEIGHT_DURATION`)

#### Recovery & Walking
- 15 min mobility & stretching
- 30–45 minutes continuous walking

---

### SUNDAY — Active Recovery
- **Base XP**: 100 XP
- **Target Attributes**: `MOB`, `END`, `FOC`
- **Scheduled Restoration**: Zero heavy lifting, zero hidden home workouts.
1. **Low-Intensity Mobility & Flexibility** — 30 minutes (`CHECK_ONLY`)
2. **Recovery Stretching & Breathing** — 15 minutes (`CHECK_ONLY`)
3. **Continuous Recovery Walk** — 45 minutes in fresh air (`TIME_BLOCK`)

## 2. Exercise Metric Types

ARCHIMEDES supports structured, typed input per movement:

| Metric Type | Inputs Displayed | Target Movements |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `WEIGHT_REPS` | Load (kg) × Repetitions | Back Squat, Bench Press, Deadlift, Rows, Presses |
| `BODYWEIGHT_REPS` | Repetitions | Push-ups, Bodyweight Squats, Back Extensions, Neck Work |
| `DURATION` | Seconds | Plank Hold, Dead Hang, Jump Rope, Reaction Drills |
| `WEIGHT_DURATION` | Load (kg per hand) × Hold Seconds | Farmer Carries |
| `ASSISTANCE_REPS` | Assistance Load (kg) × Repetitions | Assisted Pull-ups (0 kg = bodyweight) |
| `TIME_BLOCK` | Mark Completed | Continuous Recovery Walks |
| `CHECK_ONLY` | Checkbox | Mobility & Breathing Checklists |

## 3. Last Session Performance Display

When logging any movement, the interface queries prior logs and renders previous performances as direct telemetry:

```text
BARBELL BACK SQUAT
TARGET: 5 × 3–5 REPS

LAST SESSION (2026-10-07)
70 kg × 5    70 kg × 5    70 kg × 5    67.5 kg × 5    65 kg × 5

ENTER SET 1
[ KG: 72.5 ]   [ REPS: 5 ]
[ COMPLETE SET 1 ]
```

The system does not mandate weight increases; it informs the operator of previous verified loads.

---

## 4. Silent Rest Timer

- **Default Rest**: 180 seconds (3:00 minutes).
- **Controls**: `+30 SEC`, `PAUSE`, `SKIP REST`, `RESET`.
- **Background Survivability**: Uses target epoch timestamps (`targetEndTime = Date.now() + duration`) rather than simple `setInterval` increments, ensuring accurate countdowns even when the phone is backgrounded or locked.
- **Zero Sound**: Purely visual countdown without beeps, alarms, or audio cues.

---

## 5. Exception Protocols & Missed Workouts

If life events prevent training, the operator selects the appropriate protocol from the Exception Modal:

```text
WHY WAS TODAY'S MISSION NOT COMPLETED?
[ SICK ]
[ GYM CLOSED ]
[ COLLEGE EXAM ]
[ TRAVEL ]
[ INJURY / PAIN ]
[ GENUINELY UNAVOIDABLE ]
[ LOW MOTIVATION ]
[ OTHER / NORMAL MISS ]
```

1. **Sick**: 0 XP. Training paused. Consistency status maintained.
2. **Gym Closed / College Exam / Travel / Unavoidable**: Minimum viable day (10 min mobility + 20–30 min walk). Awards 75 XP. Consistency maintained.
3. **Injury / Pain**: Immediate cessation of aggravated movement. 0 XP. Consistency maintained. Clear advisory to seek medical evaluation if symptoms persist.
4. **Low Motivation (10-Minute Warm-up Rule)**:
   ```text
   10-MINUTE RULE
   Begin the warm-up. Reassess after 10 minutes.
   [ CONTINUE FULL SESSION ] -> 100% Base XP
   [ REDUCE SESSION ]        -> ~60% Base XP (Primary sets only)
   ```
5. **Other / Normal Miss**: Unexcused skip. 0 XP awarded. Both training streak and consistency status reset to 0. No cramming missed workouts into the following day.
